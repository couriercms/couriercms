# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
Welcome people of different backgrounds and experience levels, critique ideas
rather than people, respect privacy, and avoid harassment, intimidation,
discrimination, sexualized conduct, or deliberate disruption.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, issue discussions, reviews, and
other spaces where someone represents the CourierCMS project.

## Reporting and enforcement

A private conduct-reporting address and enforcement team have not yet been
established. Do not publish sensitive personal details in a public issue. This
document will be updated before the project invites broad community governance.
Repository maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect
project spaces, but this document does not promise a response time or appeal
process that does not yet exist.
